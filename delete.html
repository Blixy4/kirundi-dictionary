Account Management

Delete Account

English–Kirundi Dictionary · HelixNova™ Irreversible Action

You have the right to delete your account and all associated data at any time. This page explains how to do so — either directly from within the application, or by contacting us.

I. Delete From Within the App

The fastest way to delete your account is directly from the application. No email or external request is needed:

  1. Open the app Launch the English–Kirundi Dictionary on your device.
  2. Tap the Settings icon Tap the Settings icon (⚙) in the bottom right corner of the screen.
  3. Go to Account Tap on your account name or profile at the top of the Settings page.
  4. Open My Profile You will be taken to the My Profile page.
  5. Scroll to Danger Zone Scroll to the bottom of the page to find the Danger Zone section and tap "Delete Account".
  6. Confirm deletion Read the confirmation message and tap Confirm. The deletion is immediate and permanent.

II. Request by Email

If you are unable to access the application, you may request deletion by sending an email to:

Send your request from the email address associated with your account. Include your username in the message. We will process your request within 30 days.

helixnovaki@gmail.com

III. What is Deleted

Upon deletion, the following data is permanently removed from our systems:

Email address
Permanently deleted from authentication records.
Username
Removed from the database immediately.
Password
The stored hash is permanently erased.
Favourites
All saved favourite words are permanently removed.
Comments
Any comments submitted by the user are permanently deleted.
Contributions
Word entries or corrections contributed by the user are removed from the database.
Suggestions
Any suggestions submitted through the application are permanently removed.
Synonyms
Synonyms added by the user, if any, are deleted from the database.
Timeline
In-app deletion is immediate. Email requests are processed within 30 days.

IV. Important Notice

Before proceeding, please be aware of the following:

This action cannot be undone
  • Account deletion is permanent — it cannot be reversed or recovered.
  • You will lose access to all account-only features immediately.
  • Your email address cannot be recovered once the account is deleted.

V. Continue Without an Account

After deleting your account, you may continue using the core dictionary freely and without restriction. The following features remain fully available:

  • Looking up English and Kirundi words and definitions
  • Phonetic transcriptions and pronunciation guides
  • Full offline access — no internet required
  • Your theme, font size, preferences, and search history remain on your device — they are stored locally and are unaffected by account deletion
  • The dictionary is and will always remain free
